8 Powerful AI Tools
Built for Real Farmers

Each feature is powered by trained machine learning models and live weather data giving you actionable, accurate farming advice in seconds.

Crop Disease Detection

Upload a leaf photo and our ResNet50-based AI diagnoses the disease instantly. A Grad-CAM heatmap visually highlights the affected areas, so you can verify the AI's reasoning and act with confidence.

✓
Supports JPG, PNG, JPEG — up to 10 MB
✓
Returns disease name with confidence score
✓
Grad-CAM heatmap shows exactly where disease was detected
✓
Works in offline-poor conditions — results in seconds
Try It Now
Upload Leaf Photo

Smart Irrigation Advisor

Predict whether your crop needs irrigation right now. Our ML model weighs soil type, crop growth stage, soil moisture, and live weather to give a clear Yes/No decision with confidence score and feature importance breakdown.

✓
Auto-fills temperature & humidity from GPS weather
✓
Covers Wheat, Chilli, Potato, Carrot, Tomato
✓
8 growth stages from Germination to Harvest
✓
Shows which factors drove the irrigation decision

Fertilizer Recommendation

✓

💡 Simple as 4 Steps

Get Farming Insights
in Seconds

No agronomic expertise needed. Farmer's Assistance is designed for every farmer — just open, enter, and act.

1

Open the App & Log In

Sign up with email or Google in under 30 seconds. Your profile, preferences, and history sync securely via Supabase authentication.

2

Choose a Feature & Enter Your Data

Select from 8 feature cards on the home dashboard. Weather data auto-fills from your GPS so you rarely need to type numbers manually.

3

Get AI-Powered Insights Instantly

Receive a clear recommendation, confidence score, and feature importance breakdown — all within seconds, powered by server-side ML models.

4

Ask the AI Chatbot Anything

Still have questions? The Gemini-powered AI chatbot is available 24/7 to answer any farming question with expert, RAG-backed answers.
